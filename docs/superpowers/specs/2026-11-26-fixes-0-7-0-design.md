# The fixes of 0.7.0

Status: approved by Chris, 2026-11-26, decisions included.

## 1. Goal and decisions

0.7.0 has been tested three ways: the end-to-end run of 0.7.0 (#320), the release's defensive security review (#348), and Chris's hand tests on staging (2026-11-26, `/SWE/Kumbu/Tests/Test list 0.7.0-pre.1.md` in Kumbu², summarised in §3 so this run needs nothing outside the repositories). This run fixes the review's findings and the hand tests' findings, in one pull request, so that `v0.7.0-pre.5` comes the next day.

Decisions, each with Chris's reason:
- **One run for both.** The hand tests' findings and the review's findings share the offline code; one run means one review and one pre-release to test.
- **Fixes run on Opus, not Mythos.** Mythos is the specialist for the review itself; fixing is ordinary engineering.
- **Security fixes keep their details private** until the release, as with every version before. The private spec in `SCAI-NGO/kumbukumbu-security` describes them (§2); this spec names them by id only.
- **The hand tests' decisions are Chris's**, made while testing; each says why.

Kumbu²'s principles hold for every item: campy (notes are files and frontmatter, no stores beside them), human-centric (the screen speaks of what the person wants), invite and lead, honest (nothing is hidden quietly; honesty is part of the security architecture), and in the offline mode: **what needs a connection says so before, dimmed, not hidden, and never fails after** (F-05 of the 0.7.0 test run).

## 3. The hand tests' findings

Each with what passes. "The copy" is the offline copy in the browser; "a vault not kept" is one this device doesn't keep.

1. **F-15, an upload cut off halfway.** A new note whose upload was cut off by a lost network stays "Back online. Uploading 1 change…" for good; a reload uploads it. **Passes:** the cut-off change goes up by itself once the network is back, without a reload; `gaps #13` in `web/e2e/offline/gaps.spec.ts` loses its `test.fail` and passes.
2. **A trash against an edit is told** (decided by Chris). Neither direction loses text today (`gaps #12a`, `#12b`), but only "All changes uploaded" is shown. **Passes:** like a conflict's notice (B17's "…was also changed elsewhere…", with Open), the person learns that a note they trashed came back with an edit made elsewhere, or that a note trashed elsewhere was made again with their text; `#12a` and `#12b` assert the notice. Why: whoever trashed a note and sees it again should know why.
3. **The invitation counts as asked only once answered** (decided by Chris: "zentral"). Today it is marked asked as soon as it appears (`web/src/offline/OfflineInvite.tsx`), so an invitation never seen never comes back. **Passes:** marked on Set up or Not now; one that appeared and wasn't answered comes again at the next start. Why: an invitation must reach the person, once.
4. **Choosing vaults offline is dimmed** (decided by Chris). Today unticking a vault offline shows "The server can't be reached right now" and takes effect only at the next contact (`chooseOffline`, `web/src/offline/engines.ts`). **Passes:** offline, the vault choice, All my vaults and the budget are dimmed with "Needs a connection", as F-05's rule says; online they work as now. Why: what a device keeps is the server's word.
5. **A vault not kept shows no tree offline.** Today "DavTest is not kept on this device." stands above the vault's whole tree, and every note leads to "There is no note … on this device". **Passes:** offline, a vault not kept shows that line alone (or its tree dimmed and not clickable, the run's choice by the decision rules), never entries that lead nowhere.
6. **No error while coming back online.** Settings, Offline showed "The server can't be reached right now" just as Wi-Fi came back, while the app was still switching. **Passes:** during the switch nothing there says it failed; the bar's "Back online…" is the one word.
7. **The devices list knows this device at once.** Right after turning offline on, "Devices with a copy" doesn't list this device until a reload. **Passes:** it lists it as "This device" as soon as the copy is made.
8. **Write is dimmed in a vault not kept, offline** (decided by Chris). Today the open note of such a vault stays, and an edit there can't be left (its save has nowhere to go; the leave guard holds). **Passes:** offline, Write and the note's writing actions are dimmed with "Needs a connection" there, the note stays readable; text already being written when the connection went stays in the editor, and leaving asks Discard or Stay, never a trap. Why: dimmed, not hidden; what one reads stays.
9. **A heartbeat every 15 seconds** (decided by Chris). On real Wi-Fi, Chromium on Linux fires no `offline` or `online` event and `navigator.onLine` stays true (measured on Chris's laptop); the app notices a loss only when a request fails, so someone reading quietly sees "online" while the network is gone, and the socket dies silently. **Passes:** while the tab is visible, the app asks every 15 seconds, and at once on `navigator.connection`'s `change`; no answer within a few seconds counts as a failed request, so the offline bar comes within about 15 seconds without a click. The heartbeat keeps no idle session alive (as the socket's ticket route, a background request) and carries no secret.
10. **The offline e2e tests take the real way too.** Playwright's `setOffline` fires both events and sets `onLine` false, which real Linux Chromium doesn't. `web/e2e/support/network.ts` has `quietNetwork` (lost and back with no event) and `reconnectSlowly`. **Passes:** the tests of the mode's switch (going offline, coming back, the bar, the heartbeat of item 9) run with a quiet loss as well; with item 9, a quiet loss shows the bar without a click.
11. **Small:**
    - a reload within about a second of **Keep them here** left no copy and said nothing: the switch either finishes or says it didn't;
    - the service worker's console shows `Uncaught (in promise) TypeError: Failed to fetch` offline: it catches what it can't fetch;
    - `RekeyTests.A_crash_at_any_point_leaves_one_consistent_key` (server tests) failed once in CI on #349 and passed on a rerun: find the cause and fix it, in the code or in the test.

## 4. End-to-end example

Alice keeps Work offline on her laptop, not DavTest.
1. Reading a Work note, she turns Wi-Fi off. Within about 15 seconds, without a click, the bar says she is offline (9).
2. She opens a DavTest note that was open before: it reads, Write is dimmed with "Needs a connection" (8); the tree shows "DavTest is not kept on this device." and no entries leading nowhere (5).
3. In Settings, Offline, the vault choice is dimmed with "Needs a connection" (4).
4. She writes a new note in Work and trashes another, which Bob edits online meanwhile.
5. Wi-Fi back: nothing says it failed (6); the new note uploads even though the network went again halfway (1); the trashed note comes back with Bob's edit, and she is told so (2).

## 5. Anti-goals

- No new store, index or collection.
- Nothing about a discreet vault reaches `localStorage`, the address or the browser's history.
- No behaviour of an ordinary vault changes beyond §3 and what the private spec decides.
- No move or rename offline (0.8.0, ADR 0012).

## 6. Security boundary

- **Not touched:** `Auth/`, `CallerMiddleware`, `Crypto/` and encryption at rest, the slots, sharing and roles, sessions and the password window, the PIN check, sync, OAuth, WebDAV's authentication, and both CSPs in `src/Kumbu.Server/Api/Security.cs`.
- The exceptions are what §3 needs and what the private spec allows for its findings. They narrow what a caller can do; they never widen it.
