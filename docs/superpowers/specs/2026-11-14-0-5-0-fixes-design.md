# The fixes of 0.5.0

Status: approved by Chris, 2026-11-14; refined after an independent review the same evening (details within Chris's decisions).

## 1. Goal and decisions

One fix run before 0.5.0 takes everything that is decided:
- the findings of the security review (#201): its confidential part, and its Lows;
- the findings of the end-to-end UI tests (#200);
- what Chris found in his hand tests of `v0.5.0-pre.1` on staging.

A UI round follows on 2026-11-15, with its own spec: the explorer's toolbar, properties as their own area, the calendar on the phone, the hint about the position of frontmatter. That round is out of scope here (§9).

Decisions, each with Chris's reason:
- **One joint fix run instead of several.** Fewer merge conflicts, one review, one pre-release to test.
- **0.5.0 comes out a day or two later, done properly.** Chris: "lieber 1-2 tage später und dafür voll super".
- **Security fixes keep their details private** until the release, as with 0.4.0. The private spec in `SCAI-NGO/kumbukumbu-security` describes them; this public spec names them by id only (§3).

Kumbu²'s principles hold for every item, as in the calendar spec:
- **Campy:** notes are files and frontmatter, with no stores or layers beside them.
- **Human-centric, not process-centric:** the screen speaks of what the person wants, not of our fields.
- **Invite and lead.**
- **Honest:** nothing is hidden quietly.

## 2. Product fixes

### 2.1 The properties box folds away where the screen already says it in words (Chris's hand test)

Today the box at the top of a note shows our raw fields: `when`, `task`, `upcoming`, `tags: #flag/important` on a calendar note; `column`, `rank`, `moved { Running: 2026-11-14 }` on a board card. The header above it already says the same in words for calendar entries and flags.

- **Which notes fold:** a note whose header shows a calendar line (`EntryLine` has an entry for it) or a flag (a `flag/…` tag in the frontmatter's `tags`), or a board card (a note under `/<vault>/Board/` with a `column` key, not `Board.md` and not under `Board/Archive/`). Such a note folds **whatever other keys it has**: the box holds them all, one tap away. Every other note keeps the box as today, with no toggle.
- **Folded**, the box is one quiet line, **"Properties · N"**, N being the number of keys. A tap opens it; open, it is the box as today, with a way to fold it again. The choice is one setting per device for all such notes (`localStorage` key `kumbu.propertiesOpen`).
- **Frontmatter that can't be read** (today's `<pre>` branch) never folds.
- **Honest:** when the frontmatter was just changed by someone else (the `changed` tint, `NoteView.tsx:453`), the box shows **open**, with its tint, whatever the setting.
- **Board cards** get a line in words in the header, beside the flag and calendar lines (a component of its own next to them, `NoteView.tsx` around 409 to 410; Read mode only): "In Running since 14 Nov". The column is `column`. The date is `moved`'s entry for that column, matched ignoring case, parsed on the client from the flow map. The year is added when it isn't this year. Without a matching date it says "In Running".
- The note's text, the editor, the frontmatter and the checkpoint view (`CheckpointNote.tsx`) are untouched: this is a view.

### 2.2 The calendar line's tick is a real checkbox (Chris's hand test)

In the note header, "📅 Sun 15 Nov · 11:30 · To do · ✓ · Later": the ✓ is the button that ticks the to-do. It reads as a status, and it is too small, so Chris didn't find it.

- It becomes a **real checkbox** (`role="checkbox"`, `aria-checked`), the same component in the note header and on the week view's card, with the word beside it as its label. The target is at least 48 px.
  - **Open to-do:** ☐ To do. Ticking closes it, as today: `task: closed`, `closed:` with the date.
  - **Closed to-do:** ☑ Done, then "Closed {date}", the reason if there is one, and "Add a reason" as today. Unticking reopens it, as **Reopen** does today; the separate Reopen button goes, since the box does its job.
  - The week view's card shows ☑ for a closed to-do too, where today it shows no box.
- The separators read cleanly. The checkbox and **Later** are controls, not items between dots. The text part ("📅 Sun 15 Nov · 11:30") keeps its dots, and the controls follow after a gap.
- The e2e selectors that click `button "Done: …"` change with this. They become the checkbox, asserting the same outcome.

### 2.3 Calendar navigation (Chris's hand test)

In `web/src/calendar/CalendarPage.tsx` (the toolbar, around lines 145 to 175):

1. **Labelled buttons centred below** the week: "‹ Week 45" and "Week 47 ›"; below the month: "‹ October" and "December ›". The arrows at the top stay. This holds at every width. The ← and → keys do the same where the tree is docked (the desktop layout). They do nothing while focus is in a text field, while a dialog is open (the `topDialog()` pattern) or with a modifier key. Chris: the arrows at the top left are hard to find, and showing the week you go to is "genial".
2. **"Today" and "This week" create entries**, but they read like navigation. They become **"+ Today"** and **"+ This week"**, at least 48 px high, which also settles F-01. Chris: "+ Today" reads at a glance; "New for today" makes you wonder what is new.
3. **"Back to this week"** (or "Back to this month") shows in the top toolbar, after the arrows, when the page isn't on the current week or month, and goes there.

### 2.4 Days off (Chris, approved 2026-11-14)

- Each weekday has one of three levels: **Workday**, **Lighter day**, **Day off**.
- **Settings → Calendar**, next to the time zone: seven rows, Monday to Sunday, each with a three-way switch like "Week | Month". No hidden cycling.
- A **Day off** is shaded medium-dark and a **Lighter day** lightly, in the week view, the month list and the grid, in light and dark. Entries on those days stay fully visible, and the days stay usable (+ works as everywhere).
- **Until someone chooses**, the browser locale's weekend counts as Days off, and there are no Lighter days. The weekend comes from `locale.getWeekInfo?.() ?? locale.weekInfo`, then `.weekend` (ISO weekdays, 7 is Sunday), with `new Intl.Locale(navigator.language)`. Without either, Saturday and Sunday.
- **Stored per person** as a new field on the user's document (`UserDocument`, beside `CalendarZone`, `UserDocuments.cs:155`), through `CalendarSettings`: seven levels, Monday to Sunday. No value means "not chosen". One choice covers every vault.
- **The API:** `PUT /account/calendar` (`CalendarApi.cs:99`) takes an optional `days`. A field left out stays as it is, so saving the zone never clears the days, and the reverse. `days: null` returns to "not chosen". The server accepts exactly seven entries, each `workday`, `lighter` or `off`, and refuses anything else with 400. The answer that gives the zone gives `days` too.
- **The settings UI** (`web/src/calendar/preferences.ts` and the Settings page's calendar section): the rows show the browser's default until someone chooses. **Each tap saves at once**, and the first tap stores all seven days as shown. Below the rows, "Use my browser's weekend" returns to not chosen. It shows only while a choice is stored.
- The agents' `calendar` command doesn't show days off.
- Chris's reasons:
  - the association's statutes say it is open to people of every religion: Friday, Saturday or Sunday;
  - shift workers have other days off;
  - Chris himself works a four-day week, with Monday mostly free and Friday lighter.

### 2.5 The last note opens on start (Chris's hand test)

The installed app always started in the alphabetically first vault, because the recent notes live in memory only (`web/src/state/editor.ts:165`, `web/src/shell/MobileShell.tsx:307`).

- Per device and per user, the path of the last note opened is kept in `localStorage`, under the user's id (`kumbu.lastNote.<userId>`). On start, with no note in the address, that note opens with the tree at it (the redirect at `MobileShell.tsx:307`). If it is gone, its vault's root opens. If the vault is gone, the start is as today.
- **A note in a discreet vault is never kept.** While one is open, the kept path stays the last note of an ordinary vault (the logic of `web/src/discreet/hideAgain.ts:152`). Discreet spec §3.5: discreet paths never reach the browser's storage.
- **The kept path is removed** when its vault is no longer listed as an ordinary vault: marked discreet, here or in another session, removed, or no longer shared. It is also removed on sign-out (`TreeDrawer.tsx:363`, `Consent.tsx:74`). A session that simply ends keeps it, since it is the same person's device. Another user signing in never sees it, because the key carries the user's id.

### 2.6 Ctrl+S in Write mode (Chris's hand test)

Ctrl+S (Cmd+S on a Mac) in Write mode does what **Done** does (`endWrite`, `MarkdownEditor.tsx:278`, `editor.ts:176`): it saves and leaves Write mode. A save that fails or meets a conflict stays in Write mode, as Done does. The browser's "Save page" dialog doesn't open. While a dialog is open, and outside Write mode, the key does nothing new.

### 2.7 The UI tests' findings (#200, `docs/superpowers/overnighter/2026-11-14-ui-tests-report.md`)

- **F-03 (Medium):** a link to a heading (`[[Note#Heading]]`, with or without an alias, nested `#A#B` included) opens the note at that heading, as a block link does today. Two headings of the same name: the first wins, as `cat note#section` resolves it. A missing heading opens the note at the top with a notice, as a missing block does today, e.g. "There is no heading Roadmap in this note." The likely places are named in the report (`MarkdownView.tsx` around 233 to 238, `NoteView.tsx` `NoteAt` around 265 to 276).
- **F-01 (Low):** settled by §2.3, point 2.
- **F-02 (bug, decided by Chris):** a menu closes when the **person** scrolls, never when the tree scrolls the open note into view by itself (`web/src/components/Menu.tsx`, `web/src/shell/TreeDrawer.tsx` around 175 to 178).
- **F-04:** stays as it is (Chris): names in quotes keep a member from posing as the system, and "You" is human.
- **In the suite:** F-01's and F-03's `test.fail()` markers come out once the fix holds (F-01's is on the phone only, `calendar.spec.ts:267`). F-02 has no marker: it lives as retries around opening menus (`web/e2e/smoke/smoke.spec.ts` around 165 and 192, `web/e2e/photo/photo.spec.ts` around 29, 45 and 88, and the AI-link phone path that publishes from the note's menu). ## 3. Security fixes

## 6. End-to-end example

Alice, in the installed app on her laptop:
1. She opens the app. It shows `/Work/Pump/Certificate.md`, which she had open yesterday (§2.5), not the alphabetically first vault.
2. The note is a calendar entry: its header says "📅 Mon 16 Nov · 10:00", then, after a gap, "☐ To do   Later". Below it the properties are folded to "Properties · 3" (§2.1, §2.2).
3. She ticks ☐. It becomes ☑ Done, "Closed 16 Nov", and the note's frontmatter gains `task: closed` and `closed: 2026-11-16`, nothing else.
4. She opens the calendar. Saturday and Sunday are shaded, because her browser's locale says so; she has chosen nothing (§2.4). Below the week she sees "‹ Week 46" and "Week 48 ›" (§2.3).
5. She presses →, sees week 48, and then "Back to this week".
6. She edits a note and presses Ctrl+S: saved, and back in Read mode (§2.6).

## 7. Anti-goals

- No new store, index or collection for any of this. Days off are a field on the existing user document.
- No change to what any frontmatter write does, beyond the byte-preserving writes that exist.
- Nothing about a discreet vault reaches `localStorage`, the address or the browser's history.

## 8. Security boundary

- **Not touched:** `Auth/`, `CallerMiddleware`, `Crypto/` and encryption at rest, sharing and roles, sync, OAuth, WebDAV's authentication, and both CSPs in `src/Kumbu.Server/Api/Security.cs`.
- The exception is what the private spec allows for its findings. It narrows what a caller can do; it never widens it.
