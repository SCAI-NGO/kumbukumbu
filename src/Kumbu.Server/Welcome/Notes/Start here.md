# Start here

Kumbu² is your second brain: notes in plain Markdown that you write by hand and that your AI may read and write too. It is your memory; your AI gets a key.

## The name

*Kumbukumbu* is Swahili for memory. Kumbu × kumbu = kumbu², and the ² reads as "second", as in second brain.

## Vaults

A vault is a top-level collection of notes; this one is called "Welcome". You can have several vaults, for work and for home, say, and give each AI access to only some of them.

## Three ways in

- **By hand**, in this web app, on a phone or a laptop: [[Working by hand]].
- **Your AI**, through MCP, reads and writes the same notes: what it can do is in [[Your AI in Kumbu²]], how to set it up in [[Connect your AI]].
- **Obsidian**, kept in sync with a vault here, on your laptop or your phone: [[Obsidian]].

## Good to know

## Try it

Once your AI is connected, say to it:

- "Look in my Kumbu² Welcome vault and summarise what Kumbu² can do."
- "Make a note in my Welcome vault called 'My first note' with three things I want to remember this week."
- "Make a checkpoint of my Welcome vault, then tidy up the note 'My first note'."

## This vault is yours

Edit these notes, rename the vault or delete it (Settings → Vaults). It won't come back.
