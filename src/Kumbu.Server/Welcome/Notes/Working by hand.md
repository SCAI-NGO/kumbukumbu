# Working by hand

## Finding your way

The drawer (the button at the top left) lists your vaults and their folders and notes. Make a new vault from the drawer with **New vault**, and a new note with **New note here** in the drawer's menu.

## Reading and writing

A note opens in **Read** mode; switch to **Write** to change it. Your text saves itself as you type. If your AI or another device changed the note while you were writing, Kumbu² shows both versions and you choose: nothing is lost.

While you read a note, a bar tells you when it was changed elsewhere. In Settings, "Refresh Read mode automatically" shows such changes at once instead, with what changed tinted.

## Links

Write `[[Note name]]` to link to another note, as in Obsidian; links are looked up within the vault. `[[Note name#Heading]]` links to one section. The note's backlinks show which notes link to it.

## Search

Search finds notes by their words, and also by their meaning, so a search for "holiday plans" finds a note about "summer trip to Crete" too, even in another language.

What did a note say before? Switch on **Include earlier versions** below the search field: it also finds words that are only in an earlier version, and opens that version next to today's text.

## History

Every note keeps its last 10 earlier versions: the note's ⋯ menu → **History**. Look at any of them and restore it.

## Trash

A removed note goes to the trash and stays there for 30 days: the vault's ⋯ menu → **Trash**, where you can put it back.

## Checkpoints

A checkpoint is a named state of a note, a folder or a whole vault that never expires (up to 10 per vault, unless your server allows more): the vault's ⋯ menu → **Checkpoints**. Before restoring one, Kumbu² shows you exactly what will change.

## Bringing notes in

Upload Markdown files or whole folders with **Upload here** in the drawer's menu (or **Upload** at the bottom of the screen).

## Settings

Choose a colour scheme, dark or light, and whether Read mode refreshes by itself.
