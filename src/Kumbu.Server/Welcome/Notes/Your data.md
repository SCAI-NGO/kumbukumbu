# Your data

No lock-in: your notes are plain Markdown, and you can take them with you at any time.

## Export one vault

The vault's ⋯ menu → **Export as ZIP**. Unpacked, the folder opens in Obsidian as a vault.

## Export all your vaults

Settings → Your data → **Export my vaults…**. Untick any vault you don't want. You get one folder per vault.

## Import

Settings → Your data → **Import vaults…**. Each folder becomes a vault, or is merged into your vault of the same name. History, trash and checkpoints are not carried over: a move starts a fresh history.

## Delete

Delete a vault or your account in Settings, with your password, and the vault's name or your username typed out. How deletion works: [[Security and privacy#Deleting is for good]].

If you can't sign in any more and want your data deleted, write to the operator of your Kumbu² server.
