What’s growing next

dates are on the cautious side — we’d rather be early than late

  1. v1 · 0.1.0internally released · October ’26

    The base everything else grows from: your notes live in vaults, and any AI that speaks MCP (the common plug between AI apps and tools) can read, search and edit them.

    Everything is stored encrypted, every change lands in the history, and checkpoints take you back to a good moment. We tried it out among ourselves first.

  2. v2 · 0.2.0first public release · October ’26

    The first release for everyone. Obsidian syncs after a short pairing code, Kumbu² installs as an app and keeps itself up to date, vaults go out and come back in as a ZIP, and a welcome vault shows you around.

    You can see what your AI changed, and look into a checkpoint to take a thought back with you.

  3. v2.5 · 0.2.5Boards & attachments · coming late October / early November ’26

    A vault can become a Kanban board, a wall of columns where every card is simply a note. Markdown links count just as much as wiki links.

    And attachments your AI can actually look at: pictures and PDFs arrive as what they are, not as a pile of text.

  4. v3 · 0.3.0Think together · coming late November ’26

    Share a vault with people you trust and work in it together.

    Sharing reaches deep into how your notes are encrypted, so it gets its own security review before it ships.

  5. v4 · 0.4.0Sign in your way · coming December ’26

    More ways to sign in, and to keep your account safe: optional two-factor sign-in with your phone, passkeys instead of a password, and signing in with the account your team already uses (single sign-on).

    And support for the Open Knowledge Format, optional for a vault or a single folder, for notes that like a firmer structure.

  6. v5 · 1.0Time Machine · coming January ’27

    Your vault as it was on any day you pick, without having made a checkpoint beforehand.

Kumbu² runs on servers people host for their friends, teams and communities — ask yours, or ask us.
