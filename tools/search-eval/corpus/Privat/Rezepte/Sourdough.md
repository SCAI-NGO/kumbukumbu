# Sourdough loaf

My standard loaf, refined over a year of weekends.

## Starter

Feed 1:5:5 (starter, flour, water) the evening before. Before a holiday it goes into the fridge with a stiff feed; it survives three weeks.

## Dough

500 g bread flour, 375 g water (75 % hydration), 100 g starter, 10 g salt. Higher hydration gives a more open crumb but is harder to shape.

## Bulk fermentation

Four sets of stretch and folds, 30 minutes apart, then rest until the dough has grown by about half. At 24 °C that takes five hours.

## Baking

Preheat the Dutch oven at 250 °C for 45 minutes. Bake 20 minutes with the lid, then 25 minutes without at 230 °C.
